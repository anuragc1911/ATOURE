The brief

A brand collab. Done properly.

Chicken Shop teamed up with AToure to promote their “Seriously Good” Chicken and the launch of their new Honey Soy Wings. The goal: engage a Gen Z audience and make Chicken Shop trend across Instagram and TikTok through Manlikeisaac, integrating the brand into his short video content and 4M subscriber YouTube activation.

Isaac’s video reached nearly double the industry average engagement rate, sparking huge awareness around the Honey Soy Wings launch. The campaign reached 2.4M people with over 30K total engagements.

Campaign stats

  • Instagram views reached1M
  • TikTok community1.2M
  • YouTube views activated200K
  • Total campaign views2.4M
  • Total engagements across platforms30K+

What AToure did

Two demographics. One campaign.

Talent placement

Placed Manlikeisaac with Chicken Shop to hit two key demographics: the halal audience and London’s Gen Z youth, both communities at the core of his following.

Abdu Rozik collab

Brokered a significant collaboration with Abdu Rozik, a major talent based in Dubai with a massive following across the Middle East, amplifying the campaign’s global reach.

Full delivery

Managed the full campaign (production coordination, brand briefing, content compliance and delivery), achieving engagement nearly double the industry standard.

Key outcomes

The campaign delivered.

  • 2.4M total campaign views
  • 2× industry standard engagement rate
  • Abdu Rozik collaboration closed
  • Halal and Gen Z demographics hit
  • Honey Soy Wings product launch amplified

By pairing Manlikeisaac with Chicken Shop and bringing in Abdu Rozik from the Middle East, AToure created a campaign that resonated authentically across two distinct audiences.
