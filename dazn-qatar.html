The brief

DAZN. Qatar. Delivered.

DAZN engaged AToure to place a creator in Qatar as the exclusive live broadcaster of SLIM vs GIB on Thursday 28th November. The objective: drive live stream viewership and PPV sales through authentic creator content across Instagram, Snapchat, and TikTok, with full DAZN brand messaging throughout.

Content deliverables

  • Content before the event: stories, polls, Q&As, countdowns
  • Fight night posts: IG, Snapchat, feed posts
  • Live stream from the arena: ringside access, behind the scenes
  • Dressing room access and exclusive fighter interviews
  • Tracked links directing audience to Dazn.com for PPV

What AToure did

Creator. Brand. In Qatar.

Talent placement

Identified and placed the right creator for DAZN’s Qatar fight night, matching audience, brand tone, and platform strategy for maximum impact.

Local ops

Managed all logistics in Qatar: transport, accommodation, access credentials, ringside positioning, and dressing room coordination.

Brand compliance

Ensured all content carried DAZN messaging, tracked links, and correct tags throughout, delivered on brief across every platform.

Key outcomes

Clean execution. Every time.

  • DAZN Qatar delivered end to end
  • Live ringside content produced fight night
  • Full creator integration within DAZN’s broadcast
  • PPV tracked links across all content

AToure handled every operational detail for DAZN’s Qatar fight night, from talent placement to local logistics, delivering content on brief, on brand, and on time.
