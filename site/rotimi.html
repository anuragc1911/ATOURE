The brief

A global artist. London on his terms.

Rotimi’s UK tour required more than logistics. It needed AToure’s network to open up the city at the level his profile demands. Premium shoot locations, VIP access to London’s top hospitality venues, and full operational support for him and his team throughout the tour.
