The mission

Global attention on Africa, and a real bond with the people there.

The mission was to turn international attention toward Africa through a creator of this calibre, and to build a genuine bond with the people on the ground. Every leg protected his artistic direction while making room for real impact: ministerial and top level government meetings, sessions with Don Jazzy and Dr Sid, football with local kids, and partnerships with foundations doing lasting work.

Delivered

  • Partnerships, collaborations and sponsors
  • Media coverage
  • Visas, security and transport
  • Itinerary and experience curation in every country
