The brief

A global creator. An entire country to mobilise.

IShowSpeed’s Ivory Coast visit required building an entire operational and commercial infrastructure from zero: local sponsors, state facilitation, security forces, cultural programming, and logistics for one of the world’s biggest creators landing in West Africa with no existing local network.

AToure handled everything. Every permit. Every visa. Every vehicle. Every security operative. And the cultural moment that went viral around the world.

What AToure delivered

  • 15 state visas secured for the full touring team
  • Equipment and filming permits, all jurisdictions
  • 600+ security personnel mobilised across the city
  • Restricted zone access and privatisation of public spaces
  • 3 local sponsors sourced and closed from scratch
  • Presidential recognition for the activation

What AToure did

Every piece of the operation. Built from scratch.

Sponsors

Identified, negotiated and closed MTN, Solibra and Snedai as official local sponsors, from zero existing relationships.

Visas and entry

Secured 15 state visas and official border clearances for the full touring team.

Permits

Obtained all equipment, filming and broadcast permits across all Ivory Coast jurisdictions.

Security

Mobilised 600+ security personnel across the city to manage and control mass public gatherings throughout the tour.

Cultural moments

Mobilised Miss Ivory Coast, Magic System and Afro B. Coordinated the Zaouli dance activation that reached 55.8M Instagram views.

Full logistics

All ground transport, accommodation, hospitality, drone deployment and live stream production from start to finish, handled by AToure on the ground.

The result

One viral moment. A nation’s recognition.

The Zaouli moment

IShowSpeed’s participation in the Zaouli cultural dance became one of the most shared creator moments in West African digital history. Every logistical detail that made it possible was handled by AToure.

Presidential recognition

Baba Touré was personally endorsed by the Ivory Coast Minister of Tourism, an acknowledgement of AToure’s contribution to the country’s global cultural profile.
